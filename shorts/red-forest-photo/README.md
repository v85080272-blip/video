Факты и источники: after the 1986 accident the pine stand west of the plant received huge doses, the needles turned red-brown and the trees died within weeks (hence «Рыжий лес»); the dead trees were bulldozed and buried in trenches; young pines and birches grow there now; the Red Forest remains one of the most contaminated places in the zone. Area figures differ (≈4–10 km²), so none is named. Sources: Wikipedia «Red Forest», FAO, National Academies «Wormwood Forest», IAEA 2006 report.
