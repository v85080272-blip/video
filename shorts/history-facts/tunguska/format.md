## 0. Platform facts that shape the format (2025–26)
- **IG:** Reels can run up to 3 min. The profile grid has used 3:4 tiles since Jan 2025 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)). Mosseri's top signals are watch time, likes/reach and **sends/reach** ([Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)). Insights now has a skip-rate metric for exits in the first 3 s ([Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/why-your-reels-arent-going-viral-hook-framework)).
- **Hashtags:** max 5 per post since Dec 2025 ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)).
- **Engagement bait:** IG treats "comment a word/number/emoji" as engagement bait, and such posts may get no recommendations. Open questions are fine ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/), [IG Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232)).
- **Trial Reels** go to non-followers first. Use them to A/B-test a hook or cover ([Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/what-is-a-reel-trial-and-how-does-it-work)).
- **AI disclosure:** Meta requires a label on photoreal AI video ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/02/06/1229317971/meta-labeling-ai-generated-images-instagram-facebook-artificial-intelligence)). YouTube does the same for realistic synthetic content ([L. Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/youtube-introduces-requirements-for-ai-generated-content)).
- **YouTube monetisation:** the 15 Jul 2025 "mass-produced/inauthentic" rule targets AI-voice slideshows ([Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/)). RU media now criticise "AI history slop" ([Ruposters](https://ruposters.ru/news/22-09-2025/rasprostranyayutsya-sgenerirovannie-roliki-istorii)), and one RU Shorts author admits a made-up "history" got 26M views ([Traffhub](https://traffhub.media/blog/kejsy/kejs-400-000-podpischikov-za-4-mesyacza-v-youtube-s-pomoshhyu-shorts/)). **Checked facts and real archival inserts are the differentiator.**
- **RU audience:** IG is blocked in RF and reached via VPN. Paid ads and integrations on IG have been banned since 1 Sep 2025 ([Callibri](https://callibri.ru/blog/zapret-reklamy-v-instagram)). Mirror each episode to VK Клипы, YouTube Shorts and Telegram.

## 1. What top history-fact reels look like
**Length:** 35–60 s for one story; 15–25 s for a single-fact clip. Short clips win on completion; go longer only if every second adds something new ([Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length), [Klap](https://klap.app/blog/how-long-should-reels-be)). Tunguska target: 50–58 s.

| s | Beat | Tunguska |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Cold open on the most extreme frame, with the year/number on screen. Voice and an impact hit land on frame 1. No logo, no «привет» | fireball over taiga, «1908» |
| 1.5–4 | Open loop (an unanswered question) | «…и спорят об этом до сих пор» |
| 4–12 | Place + person (map zoom, witness) | Ванавара, Семёнов на крыльце |
| 12–35 | 3–4 escalating specifics, each with a number | жар в 65 км → вывал леса → светлые ночи над Лондоном |
| ~25–30 | Mid re-hook | «Но самое странное нашли через 19 лет» |
| 35–50 | Payoff/twist | ни кратера, «телеграфный лес», версии |
| 50–56 | Open question + a loop line that flows back into frame 1; cut right after it | «Так что же взорвалось над Сибирью в 1908-м?» |

- **Hook and ending:** viewers decide within 1–3 s, and you shouldn't drag the ending ([Habr: 100 000 Shorts](https://habr.com/ru/amp/publications/810947)). Pace matters almost as much as topic ([SMMplanner](https://smmplanner.com/blog/korotkiie-vidieo-kotoryie-smotriat-do-kontsa/amp/)). The hook must work on mute, so put it on screen as text ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/what-makes-a-reel-hook-viral)).
- **Loop:** combine a script loop (the last line completes the first) with a visual loop (last frame ≈ first frame) ([faceless.so](https://faceless.so/blog/looping-short-videos-tutorial-story-seamless-loops)).
- **Cut pace:** change the visual every ~1.5–3 s ([Aibrify](https://aibrify.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-curve-playbook)), but every cut must add information. A 2026 experiment found that faster pacing alone *lowered* engagement ([paper](https://archives.marketing-trends-congress.com/2026/pages/PDF/paper_professor_DOST_HUANG.pdf)). That works out to about 18–25 shots per 55 s.
- **Narration:**
  - Deep male documentary voice, short sentences (≤12 words), present tense («Утро. Ванавара. Семёнов сидит на крыльце»).
  - Pace: the English norm is 140–160 wpm ([FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/words-per-minute-speaking)). Russian words are longer, and average monologue speech runs ≈120 wpm ([МГУ, Ломоносов-2018](https://aspirant.msu.ru/archive/Lomonosov_2018/data/12832/75403_uid70902_report.pdf)). Aim for **2.3–2.6 words/s (≈140–155 wpm)**, with 0.3–0.5 s pauses before reveals.
  - **script_v1 has 153 words, about 59–66 s at that pace.** Trim to ≈130 words, or set TTS speed to ×1.05–1.1, to hit 55 s.
- **Visual mix:**
  - **≈70% AI cinematic stills.** Keep one look throughout: period-correct 1908, film grain, desaturated palette plus fire-orange. Animate them with 2.5D parallax or a slow push-in (depth map → parallax: [DepthFlow](https://pypi.org/project/depthflow), [Immersity AI](https://www.toolsforhumans.ai/ai-tools/immersity-ai)). Depth Anything V2 and RealVisXL are already in `/home/user/work/models`.
  - **2–3 hero shots as image-to-video:** the flash, and the blast wave flattening the forest.
  - **10–15% real archival photos,** captioned «настоящее фото 1927» (Kulik's expedition, fallen trees). This builds trust.
  - **≈10% map/3D:** globe zoom to Vanavara, a 65 km ring, the treefall zone laid over Moscow (Blender).
  - **Number cards** with animated counters: 65 км / 2 000 км² / 19 лет.
  - **Don't** create fake "archive photos" with AI, and check costumes and buildings for anachronisms.
- **Subtitles (1080×1920):**
  - **Font:** Montserrat ExtraBold/Black, or Manrope/Inter ExtraBold (all have Cyrillic), at 80–96 px. White with a 6–8 px black stroke or soft shadow. The active word is yellow #FFD400, or fire-orange for this episode. Always highlight numbers ([Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention), [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/social-media-captions-styles-a-complete-guide/)).
  - **Chunking:** 1–3 words per chunk (Russian words are long), max 2 lines. Word-by-word karaoke highlight, leading the audio by ~0.1 s ([Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices)).
  - **Position:** block centred at y≈1150–1250 (60–65% of height), ≤820 px wide (x≈130–950). Keep clear of the Reels UI: top ≈220–270 px; bottom ≈450 px (up to 35% ≈ 670 px with an expanded caption); right ≈120–150 px for the buttons ([somake.ai RU](https://www.somake.ai/ru/blog/instagram-reel-size-guide), [Hopper](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/), [House of Marketers](https://www.houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories)). Raise the block if it covers a face.
- **Sound:**
  - **Effects:** an impact on frame 0, then a low drone plus taiga ambience (wind, birds, fire crackle). Whooshes start 10–20 ms before each cut, and a riser leads into a hit on every reveal. Leave ~0.5 s of silence before the key line («А до взрыва — 65 километров»).
  - **Mix:** music ≈18–20 dB under the voice, with 6–12 dB ducking (attack 30–80 ms) ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/audio-ducking-for-clear-dialogue-in-video), [Nemo](https://www.nemovideo.com/blog/ai-video-editing/how-to-balance-voiceover-and-background-music)). Master ≈ −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP. Use dark-ambient cinematic music, not a trending pop track.

## 2. Covers that work
| Surface | Centre crop of a 1080×1920 cover | Visible y-range |
|---|---|---|
| IG profile grid (since Jan 2025), TikTok grid | 3:4 = 1080×1440 | 240–1680 |
| IG home feed | 4:5 = 1080×1350 | 285–1635 |
| Square surfaces (cross-posts, older crops) | 1:1 = 1080×1080 | 420–1500 |
| IG Reels tab / full-screen | 9:16 | 0–1920 |

- **Universal text-safe box: x 80–1000, y 420–1500.** Put the headline at y≈440–820 and the focal face/object at y≈820–1450. Keep the tile's bottom-left corner free for the view-count badge ([Hopper](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/), [Socialk](https://socialk.it/en/sizes/instagram-reel-size), [Nemo/TikTok](https://www.nemovideo.com/blog/tiktok-thumbnail-guide)).
- **Uploading:** upload a custom 1080×1920 cover and check «Crop profile image». You can change the cover after posting ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-cover-photo/)).
- **Shorts and TikTok:** custom Shorts thumbnails are still rolling out (desktop and YPP channels first, [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/)). So make **frame 1 of the video the cover itself**. That also keeps feed autoplay seamless.
- **Words:** 2–4 (≤5). Thumbnails with 1–5 words beat those with 11+ ([WaveGen](https://wavegen.ai/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices), [Miracamp](https://www.miracamp.com/learn/youtube/how-to-design-thumbnails)).
- **Type:** condensed heavy CAPS with Cyrillic: Bebas Neue (free, [FontLibrary](https://fontlibrary.org/en/font/bebasneueregular)), Oswald 700 or Druk Cyr, at 150–230 px. Set «1908» even bigger, as a graphic element.
- **Contrast:** white/yellow text with a thick shadow on a dark sky. One accent colour (fire-orange) and one focal point. Test at 25% size, since a grid tile is ≈⅓ of the screen width.
- **Faces and eyes:** faces with emotion lift CTR (vendor data: 6.8% vs 4.1%, [WaveGen](https://wavegen.ai/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices)). Options: a witness's face lit orange, with the eye-line pointing at the flash; or a tiny human silhouette against a giant fireball for scale.
- **Arrows/red circles:** common practice, but no source tested them. Use at most one, e.g. a circle around a «телеграфный» tree.
- **Curiosity gap:** a contradiction or question the image doesn't answer. The spoken hook then pays it off with a *new* detail, so don't read the cover aloud.
- **Series look:** use one template every episode (year position, colour bar, «ТАЙНЫ ИСТОРИИ · №1») so the grid reads as a collection.

## 5. Ten follow-up episodes (light, verifiable, visual, with a hero)
1. **Сихотэ-Алинский метеорит, 1947.** «Через 39 лет после Тунгуски с неба упал железный дождь — и его нашли.» An eyewitness artist's painting ended up on a 1957 stamp ([Calend](https://www.calend.ru/events/5886/)).
2. **Гром-камень, 1768–70.** «Скалу весом около 1500 тонн доставили в Петербург без единого мотора.» ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Horseman))
3. **Пётр I на верфи, 1697.** «Русский царь назвался Петром Михайловым и пошёл плотником на голландскую верфь.» ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Embassy_of_Peter_the_Great))
4. **Ледяной дом, 1740.** «Императрица построила дворец изо льда — со слоном, который извергал горящую нефть.» ([ЭСБЕ](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/%D0%AD%D0%A1%D0%91%D0%95/%D0%9B%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%8F%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B9_%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BC))
5. **Резанов и Кончита, 1806** (the real «Юнона и Авось»). «Русский дипломат приплыл в Калифорнию за хлебом, а нашёл невесту.» ([NPS](https://www.nps.gov/prsf/learn/historyculture/love-story.htm))
6. **Прокудин-Горский, 1909–15.** «Это не раскраска: цветные фото царской России, снятые больше ста лет назад.» ([LoC](https://loc.gov/exhibits/empire/gorskii.html))
7. **Янтарная комната.** «Подарок прусского короля Петру I пропал в 1945-м — его ищут до сих пор.» ([History Hit](https://www.historyhit.com/facts-about-the-lost-amber-room/))
8. **«Челюскин», 1934.** «Пароход раздавило льдами, 104 человека остались на льдине — и спасли всех.» ([БРЭ](https://bigenc.ru/c/cheliuskin-parokhod-12681d))
9. **Белка, Стрелка и Пушинка, 1960–61.** «Щенок собаки-космонавта переехал в Белый дом — к дочери Кеннеди.» ([JFK Library](https://www.jfklibrary.org/learn/about-jfk/life-of-john-f-kennedy/fast-facts-john-f-kennedy/pets-in-the-whitehouse))
10. **Леонов, 1965.** «Первый человек в открытом космосе едва не застрял снаружи — а потом сел в тайгу.» ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voskhod_2))

## Fact flags for script_v1 (found while researching)
- **«80 млн деревьев»:** Boslough et al. call this a myth (it comes from an early area estimate) ([GSA 2024](https://www.unm.edu/~mbeb/Abstracts/GSA-Tunguska-2024.pdf)). 2 150 km² is the area where *any* treefall was noted ([arXiv 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.05484)). Say «вывал леса — больше 2000 км²», and keep «сравнимо с Москвой» (2 561 km²).
- **Bright nights:** glowing night skies over London are confirmed ([Cornell](https://news.cornell.edu/node/273080), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Tunguska-event)). Reading a newspaper at midnight has no primary source. Hedge it («по рассказам, можно было читать газету») or drop it.
- **Semenov:** "as if my shirt was on fire" and being thrown off the porch at Vanavara are confirmed. Translations differ on how far («несколько метров» vs ~2 m) ([Slate](https://www.slate.com/blogs/the_vault/2013/02/15/tunguska_event_eyewitness_accounts_from_the_last_time_a_heavenly_body_burned.html), [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunguska_event)). «Сбросило с крыльца» is the safest wording.
- **St Petersburg:** never use "4 h 47 min later it would have hit St Petersburg". It's a myth ([GSA 2024](https://www.unm.edu/~mbeb/Abstracts/GSA-Tunguska-2024.pdf)).
- **London distance:** ≈5 700 km from the site (great-circle). Energy is 3–50 Mt ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunguska_event)), so «в сотни раз мощнее Хиросимы» is safe.
